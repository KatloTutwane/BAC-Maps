    Controls

    WASD move the marker (relative to the screen). Shift sprints. E uses stairs. In Third-person, W/S walk forward and back and A/D turn. F follow camera. I ground-floor cutaway and back, PgUp/PgDn next floor up or down. R whole campus, as drawn. [ ] turn the map. Esc closes or cancels. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, Shift+drag, right-drag, Shift+scroll or a two-finger twist to turn the map. The N button shows north; click it to turn back to the drawing's view. In Third-person the map turns so you always walk up the screen. Tap anything on the drawing for its details and actions. To pick a first floor spot, show "First floor" only.
